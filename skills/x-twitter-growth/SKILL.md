# X/Twitter Growth Engine

Build audience, craft viral content, and analyze engagement with a systematic 6-step framework.

## Core Insight

> "Replies and engagement are 50%+ of growth, not just posting."

The X algorithm rewards **replies over likes**. Content that drives conversation grows faster than content that gets passive likes.

## The 6-Step Framework

### 1. Profile Audit
- Bio clarity: Does it communicate value in 1 sentence?
- Pinned tweet: Best-performing content or strong hook?
- Posting frequency: Consistent or sporadic?
- Follower-to-following ratio health

### 2. Competitive Intelligence
Analyze 5-10 successful accounts in your niche:
- What formats get the most **replies** (not likes)?
- What time do they post?
- What topics consistently perform?
- What conversation gap can you own?

### 3. Content Creation

**Atomic tweets** (single insight, high RT potential):
```
[Counterintuitive claim]
[One-line explanation]
[Proof or example]
```

**Threads** (best format for growth):
```
Tweet 1: Hook — bold claim or surprising stat
Tweets 2-N: Proof, examples, numbered steps
Last tweet: CTA + summary
```

**Strategic replies**: Reply to accounts with 10x+ your following to borrow their audience.

### 4. Algorithm Mechanics

**X rewards:** replies, time-on-page, bookmarks, quote tweets

**X penalizes:**
- Links in tweet body → always put links in the **first reply**
- Hashtag overuse → max 1-2, only if highly relevant
- Editing a tweet immediately after posting

### 5. Growth Playbook (8-week progression)

| Week | Focus | Daily actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Foundation | 2-3 tweets/day, 10 strategic replies |
| 3-4 | Consistency | First thread, engage trending topics in niche |
| 5-6 | Amplification | Collab threads, quote tweet strategy |
| 7-8 | Scaling | Daily threads, build reply community |

### 6. Content Calendar

| Followers | Posts/day | Threads/week |
|---|---|---|
| < 1K | 2-3 | 1 |
| 1K-10K | 3-4 | 2-3 |
| 10K-50K | 3-5 | 3-4 |
| 50K+ | 2-5 | 4-5 |

## NEVER

- Put links in tweet body (kills reach — always first reply)
- Use generic hashtags (#marketing, #success, #entrepreneur)
- Post-and-ghost — reply to every comment for 30 min after posting
- Buy followers (destroys engagement rate permanently)
- Post identical content across multiple accounts (shadow ban risk)
